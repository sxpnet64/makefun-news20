Lead story · Weather

Akvan comes ashore on India’s east coast

Cyclone Akvan began its landfall early on Friday, crossing the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast near Gopalpur after weakening from a severe cyclonic storm. Winds of 70–80 km/h, gusting to 90, drove heavy rain over the coastal districts.

About 300,000 people were moved to safety in Odisha, with 60 state and 14 national rescue teams deployed; 41 trains were cancelled and 29 rescheduled. — IMD via ETV Bharat, The Hindu, 20 Nov
Diplomacy

Three leaders, one declaration — and no peace deal

Trump, Xi and Putin met for 81 minutes in Shenzhen and issued a four-point “peace declaration” on the UN, energy, courts and nuclear risk — with no mention of Ukraine or Iran. Putin then ruled out an energy or Black Sea truce after Ukraine’s third strike on the Crimea bridge this week, vowing a harsher reply.

Sources: Reuters, Dawn, The Independent, 19–20 Nov
Washington

Trump fires his cyber chief after a run of outages

National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross was fired after outages hit an FAA ground stop, Microsoft Outlook, United Airlines, banks and an NSA network. The Pentagon’s chief information officer, Kirsten Davies, takes over in an acting capacity.

Sources: CNN, AFP, Washington Examiner, 19–20 Nov
Markets

Wall Street rout deepens as Dow loses 1,101 points

The Dow fell 2.19% to 49,097, its first close below 50,000 since June. The S&P 500 slid 2.02% for a sixth straight loss and the Nasdaq 2.25%, as tech shares sold off — even as Anthropic’s Nasdaq debut closed 17% above its $83 IPO price.

Sources: Newspim, NDTV Profit, 19–20 Nov
Asia

Nikkei dives 4.16% as the sell-off spreads

Asian markets fell hard on Friday, tracking Wall Street: Japan’s Nikkei dropped 4.16%, South Korea’s Kospi 3.71% and Australia’s ASX 1.13%. Japan’s inflation rose to 2.3% in October, its highest in a year.

Source: NDTV Profit, 20 Nov
Technology

US cleared nearly 900,000 AI chips for China

The Trump administration approved shipments of almost 900,000 advanced Nvidia and AMD AI chips to Chinese firms from December 2025 to May 2026, the House Foreign Affairs Committee said — a disclosure that lands as Washington tightens tech curbs elsewhere.

Sources: The Standard, New Straits Times, 20 Nov
Middle East

Houthis strike four targets across Saudi Arabia

Yemen’s Houthis said they hit a Riyadh financial district — damaging the capital’s tallest skyscraper, the sovereign wealth fund’s headquarters, with no casualties — plus the main airport, a Jubail petrochemical plant and an air base, warning foreign investors to leave.

Sources: TASS via Stockbiz, The Standard, 19–20 Nov
